B. Suggestions for Specific Points to Cover in the Literature Review Section or sub-section: 
• Try to stick to about two single-spaced pages (assuming a paper in the range of 20 
pages). 
• This section should consist of two parts (both of which should be brief).  
o The first section should discuss previous research directly relevant to your paper 
(not every single paper written on the topic).  
o The review needs to be topical and include research that employs similar 
methods to the ones you use, analyzes a similar model, uses the same 
dataset, etc. 
o Keep in mind that your main contribution will be to the economics 
discipline. 
o The second section should explain your contribution in more detail.  
o You should discuss how your approach is different from what has been 
done before:  
o Is it new data?  
o A new model?  
o A new identification strategy?  
o Are you answering a question more broadly/specifically?  
o How are you improving on a previous paper?  
o In this section, you should think creatively about external validity 
issues: Are your findings relevant for a population/institutional 
environment that is different from previous work, and could this be 
the reason your findings differ? 
• Let me emphasize the previous point again: your main contribution will be judged in 
relation to previous economics papers and the economics discipline.  
o This statement implies you should be able to relate your work mostly to previous 
economics peer-reviewed papers!  
o If you are working on an interdisciplinary topic (such as health), it is fine to cite 
several papers from another discipline (e.g., epidemiology) that examine a related 
question. However, mostly your work will be judged based on its relation to 
previous economics papers and how it improves our understanding of economic 
behavior relative to previous economics work.  
o So, be sure that your literature review mostly consists of papers from peer-
reviewed economics journals.2 
 
 
 
2 A fairly comprehensive list of peer-reviewed economics journals is available at: http://www.aeaweb.org/econlit/journal_list.php
