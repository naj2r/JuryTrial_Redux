B. Suggestions for Specific Points to Cover in the Identification Strategy Section. 
o Report interesting stylized facts or patterns from the table with your descriptive 
statistics. Focus on pointing out patterns that may provide useful background to your 
audience to understand your analysis better. Provide context, describe the 
relationship(s) between key variables, and help the reader understand your subsequent 
results and get a better sense of issues related to generalizability.  
o Typical summary statistics that are offered include average and SD values for each 
variable.  
o Histograms and other figures are acceptable, albeit not standard. Such data 
presentation could be effective for critical variables that exhibit skewed 
distributions. This may be a relevant consideration for robustness checks, or 
lack thereof, of the empirical method or the empirical approach related to the 
main hypotheses.
