The main result of the paper is that electoral sorting improves the identification of the high-skilled individuals. Pri-
vate market sorting for the incumbent and the subordinate reduces the set of environments under which the main
result holds. Rather, there is tension between electoral sorting and market sorting. Introducing private market sort-
ing expands the set of economic environments where the election mechanism is an effective tool. Therefore, the main
result is reasonably robust to the inclusion of outside labor market opportunities for both the boss and the subordinate.
